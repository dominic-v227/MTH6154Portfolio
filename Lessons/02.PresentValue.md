## Solved exercises

### Exercise 3 (from Exercise Sheet 2)
You buy a car with a loan of 
£12,000, to be repaid in 36 equal monthly instalments, the first one in a month’s time. The interest rate is 
6%, compounded monthly. Calculate the monthly instalment.
Firstly, as the interest rate is compounded monthly, you need to convert to the monthly rate. 6% is the the nominal annual rate. Knowing this, the effective monthly rate is: i=0.06/12=0.005. Therefore, the monthly discount factor= 1/1+i=\(\beta\). The present value is P=\(\beta\)+P**^2+...P**^36 which is a geometric series. P=the (first ratio- last discount monthly factor*ratio)/(1-ratio). This is equal to 12000=P((\beta\)-\(\beta\)^37)/(1-\(\beta\) To make P the subject: P=12000(1-\(\beta\))/(\(\beta\)=\(\beta\)^37) Subbing in \(\beta\)=1/1-0.005: P=£365.06=montly installment
### Exercise 4a
A perpetuity pays 2500
 every year. The effective annual interest rate is 
4%. Calculate its present value if the first payment is in one year's time
As the first payment is received at the end of the first year, it's an ordinary perpetuity. The present value is calculated by dividing the annual payment by the effective annual interest rate through PV=C/R= £2500/0.04=£62,500. 
### Exercise 4b
Calculate its present value if the first payment is today
If the first payment is today, it is received immediately. Therefore, there's an extra payment today which is immediately payed (£2500)+62,500 from 4a. So, the present value=£65000.
### Exercise 4c
Calculate its present value if the first payment is in 10 year's time
In 10 year's time, the perpetuity has the same value as in 4a when calculated at the end of the 9th year. This is due to the first pyament being received one year later so it must be discoutned 9 years back. So, PV= 62500/((1.04)^9)= £43,911.67
