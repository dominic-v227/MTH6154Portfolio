### Exercise 3 (from Exercise Sheet 2)
You buy a car with a loan of 
£12,000, to be repaid in 36 equal monthly instalments, the first one in a month’s time. The interest rate is 
6%, compounded monthly. Calculate the monthly instalment.
FIrstly, as the interest rate is compounded monthly, you need to convert to the monthly rate. 6% is the the nominal annual rate. Knowing this, the effective monthly rate is: i=0.06/12=0.005. Therefore, the monthly installment will be 12000(1+0.005)^12=£12740.13
